How It Works

From Raw Wattage to Actionable Insight

EnergyIQ runs a tight loop — sense, record, display, control — every 5 seconds.

01
Sense Device Watts
Every device reports a live fluctuating wattage reading based on its rated base consumption ± natural variation.
02
Lock Into History
Each 5-second reading is permanently frozen into a timestamped history slot. Past data is never changed or regenerated.
03
Render the Graph
The stock-style chart grows left-to-right. Only the live tip moves. Zoom in to see second-level micro-variations clearly.
04
AI Status & Alerts
Rooms over 85% capacity trigger critical alerts with estimated waste costs. You get actionable tips, not just raw numbers.
